Refund Protocols

DIRECTIVE: FIN-992-ALPHA
ADVISORY: Digital assets are classified as "Consumable Intel". Once accessed, they cannot be un-accessed. Read protocols carefully.

1. THE "LABOR SHIELD"

MH STUDIOS separates charges into two categories: Intel (Product) and Operations (Labor).

  • Labor is Non-Refundable: Time spent by Freelancers or Contractors on research, coding, or design is compensable even if the Mission is cancelled.
  • If a Mission is cancelled mid-operation, you will be refunded for the remaining unallocated budget only.

2. THE 72-HOUR INSPECTION WINDOW

Upon delivery of the Mission Objectives (Files/Code), the Operative has 72 Hours (3 Days) to inspect the intel.

  • Silence is Acceptance: If no "Dispute Signal" is fired within 72 hours, the system auto-classifies the Mission as COMPLETE.
  • Once marked COMPLETE, funds are released to the Freelancer and cannot be reversed.

3. DIGITAL ASSET LOCK

Due to the nature of digital warfare, the following assets are FINAL SALE once downloaded:

  • Source Code (.cpp, .js, .py)
  • 3D Models (.obj, .vxl)
  • High-Resolution Schematics (PDF, PNG)

Exception: If the file is corrupted or proven malicious, a replacement will be issued immediately.

4. WALLET RETENTION PROTOCOL

In the event of an approved refund, MH STUDIOS reserves the right to issue the refund as "Armory Credits" (Wallet Balance) to ensure rapid redeployment of funds.

Cash refunds to original payment methods (Bank/Card) are subject to a 5% Processing Fee and may take 5-10 business days depending on civilian banking sectors.

5. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

If an Operative and a Freelancer disagree on the quality of intel:

  1. Phase 1: Mutual Negotiation (Private Chat).
  2. Phase 2: High Command Intervention. MH STUDIOS Admins will review the brief vs. the result.
  3. Verdict: The Admin's decision is final and binding.
