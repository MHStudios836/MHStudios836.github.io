Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard Protocols

AES-256 Encryption
All financial data transmitted through MH STUDIOS is protected using TLS 1.2+ (Transport Layer Security) and military-grade AES-256 encryption. We utilize a secure handshake protocol to ensure no data is intercepted during transit.
Data Tokenization
We do not store your credit card numbers. Instead, we use a process called "Tokenization." We replace your sensitive data with a unique, randomized token. The actual data is held securely in the vaults of our payment processors (Stripe/PayPal).
Restricted Access
Access to billing information is strictly limited to authorized personnel with Level 4 Security Clearance. Every access attempt is logged, monitored, and audited in real-time to prevent unauthorized internal breaches.
