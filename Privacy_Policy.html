Privacy Protocols

DATA CLASSIFICATION: CONFIDENTIAL

1. INTEL COLLECTION (Data We Gather)

To execute missions effectively, MH STUDIOS collects specific data points from Operatives:

  • Identity Data: Name, University affiliation, and Academic Major.
  • Contact Data: Secure email addresses and phone numbers for mission alerts.
  • Operational Data: Mission briefs, file attachments (PDF/ZIP), and chat logs.
  • Technical Data: IP addresses and device types (for security auditing).

2. PROTOCOL USAGE (How We Use It)

Your data is utilized strictly for the following operational parameters:

  • Mission Execution: Connecting students with freelancers to complete academic tasks.
  • Communications: Sending real-time updates regarding mission status.
  • System Integrity: Preventing unauthorized access and fraud detection.

We do not sell Operative data to civilian marketing agencies.

3. EXTERNAL ALLIANCES (Third Parties)

We utilize trusted third-party infrastructure to maintain system uptime:

  • Firebase (Google): For secure database hosting and authentication.
  • Stripe/PayPal: For processing financial logistics.
  • GitHub Pages: For frontend deployment.

4. DIGITAL TRACERS (Cookies)

We use "Cookies" (small data beacons) to:

  • Keep you logged in during a session.
  • Remember your dashboard preferences.
  • Analyze traffic patterns to optimize server load.

You may disable tracers in your browser settings, but this may cause system instability.

5. DEFENSE SYSTEMS (Security)

We employ SSL Encryption and Level-4 Firewalls to protect your data. While no system is impenetrable, we adhere to industry-standard PCI-DSS Protocols to minimize risk.
